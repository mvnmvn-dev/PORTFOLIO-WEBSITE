05 / Common questions

Good questions make better projects.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. I explain options in everyday language and point out the trade-offs before we choose.

Who owns the code, design, and data?

Ownership and access are agreed in writing before work begins, so there are no surprises at handover.

What happens after the project launches?

You receive a handover with the important links and instructions. Follow-up fixes or maintenance are scoped separately.

Can you update or expand my system later?

Yes. I can review the current system, explain the safest next step, and quote the change before starting.

How will we communicate?

We agree on a main channel and a regular update rhythm. I keep decisions and open questions visible.

What do you need from me to get started?

Start with the goal, who uses the system, what feels difficult today, and any examples you already have.
