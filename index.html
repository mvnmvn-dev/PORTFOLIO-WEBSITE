05 / Common questions

Good questions make better projects.

Do I need to know anything technical?

Not at all. Most people I work with aren’t technical. I explain things in plain English or Filipino, and when there’s a choice to make, I tell you what I’d pick and why.

Who owns the code, design, and data?

You get the full source code at turnover, along with training so your team knows how to use it. Anything else, like design files and data access, we write down before I start.

What happens after the project launches?

At turnover I hand over the links, logins, and training. Then you check the system and I make revisions based on your feedback. Bigger changes or ongoing maintenance we agree on separately, with a price first.

Can you update or expand my system later?

Yes. Send me what you have and I’ll go through it first. Then I’ll tell you what is safe to change and what it would cost before I touch anything. It doesn’t have to be a system I built.

How will we communicate?

Whatever is easiest for you: Messenger, Google Meet, Discord, or email. I’ll send regular updates and write down decisions so nothing gets lost. I reply within 1–2 business days.

What do you need from me to get started?

A rough idea of what you want, who will use it, and what is slow or annoying today. Screenshots, spreadsheets, or the forms you use now help a lot. It’s fine if it’s messy. That’s what the first call is for.
